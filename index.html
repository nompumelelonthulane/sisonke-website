Our mission
"Financial freedom shouldn't depend on where you were born or what bank approved you."

Sisonke — meaning "we are together" in isiZulu — was born from a simple truth: millions of South Africans are locked out of the digital economy not because they lack resources, but because the system was never designed for them.

We're building the financial infrastructure that puts community first. From Soweto to Limpopo, from Khayelitsha to Durban — every South African deserves a safe, fast, and affordable way to manage their money.
